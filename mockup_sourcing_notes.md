# Demo catalog mockup sourcing

## Current demo product

The published tenant demo currently exposes one product, **candy**, with a pouch-like placeholder card. Its upgraded visual should therefore be a premium snack or candy pouch, not an unrelated device or furniture mockup.

## Reviewed free sources

| Source | Relevant asset direction | Stated terms | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Mockups Design — Pouch mockup](https://mockups-design.com/pouch-mockup/) | Four modern pouch angles, including a movable composition | Commercial use; attribution not required; private and commercial purpose | Preferred source family for visual consistency and explicit terms. |
| [Mockups Design — Food pouch mockup](https://mockups-design.com/free-food-pouch-mockup/) | Food-pouch presentation suitable for the candy demo | Source family states commercial use and no attribution on its individual pouch pages; final page asset to be verified before use | Candidate for the primary card visual. |
| [Unblast — Snack pouch](https://unblast.com/free-snack-pouch-mockup-psd/) | Two-pouch top-view snack composition applicable to candy | Personal and commercial use | Candidate for a supporting or alternate catalog composition. |
| [Good Mockups — Snack bag](https://goodmockups.com/free-potato-chips-snack-bag-packaging-mockup-psd/) | Square snack-pouch shot with editable background | Free for personal and commercial use | Candidate only if its preview delivers a better card crop. |
| [ResourceBoy — Standing food pouch](https://resourceboy.com/mockups/front-view-of-standing-food-pouch-bag-mockup/) | Front-facing standing pouch | Personal and commercial use | Reserve source; use only after confirming the destination asset permits direct preview use. |

## Selection rule

Only a source with explicit commercial-use terms will be used. The live website will receive a locally hosted, optimized image asset, not a PSD file and not a third-party hotlink. Any design art on a purchased or displayed pouch stays unbranded so the demo does not imply ownership of a source creator’s label artwork.
