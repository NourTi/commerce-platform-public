# UI guidance sources for the commerce-platform reset

## UI/UX Pro Max

UI/UX Pro Max describes itself as a design-intelligence skill with a design-system generator that combines product-type matching, style recommendations, palette selection, page patterns, typography pairing, anti-pattern filtering, and a pre-delivery checklist. Its published rules cover developer tools, B2B products, and e-commerce, making it applicable to Forge’s dual public-storefront and admin-platform surfaces.

The guidance most relevant to this reset is pragmatic: designs must work with natural heading wrapping; text must reflow at narrow widths and scaling; badges cannot convey meaning through color alone; interactive tags need native semantics, focus indicators, and programmatic state; and reduced-motion preferences must be respected.

Source: https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill
